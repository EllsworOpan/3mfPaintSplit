FROM MODEL TO PRINT

A clean cut, with the color intact.

  1. Open a painted 3MF. Native PrusaSlicer MMU and Bambu / Orca AMS paint are read, including paint inside individual triangles.
  2. Position the plane. Choose X, Y, or Z, change the offset, or tilt and rotate the plane. Drag the arrow to move it; switch to Rotate for angle handles.
  3. Apply your cut. Both pieces remain. Select any piece and cut it again. Undo keeps the last 12 cuts.
  4. Save the painted 3MF. Import it into PrusaSlicer as separate objects, keep the same material slot order, choose your printer and filaments, then orient each piece for printing.

New interior faces use the material you choose. Existing paint is resolved into colored mesh triangles, so exports can be larger than the original.

Printer profiles, supports, seams, modifiers, connectors, and variable layer settings are not carried over. Cuts require closed, consistently oriented meshes. This is a straight plane cutter; it does not add pins or joints.

All processing happens on your device. Use F to fit, C to cut, and Ctrl / ⌘ Z to undo.
